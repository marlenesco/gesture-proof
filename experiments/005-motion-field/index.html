Question

Can palm velocity drive an effect through a clean signal boundary?

02

Hypothesis

Stable ownership, elapsed-time velocity, smoothing, jump rejection, and confirmed gesture modes should create a field that is expressive without exposing tracker internals.

03

Deterministic evidence

  • Direction reverses without a frame-count dependency.
  • Stillness remains below motion hysteresis.
  • Long gaps and impossible jumps restart acquisition.
  • Short dropout freezes velocity; long dropout expires.
  • One owner remains stable when a second hand appears.

Not yet proven

Decision / revise
