01

Question

Can gestures manipulate a small wireframe scene without transform jumps or clutter?

02

Hypothesis

Locked actions, explicit release, baseline reacquisition, and strict object caps should keep movement causal and recoverable.

03

Mappings

  • Pinch + palm displacement translates X/Y.
  • Fist + palm displacement rotates pitch/yaw.
  • Two-hand span ratio scales around selected center.
  • Open palm, point, ambiguity, and dropout transform nothing.
